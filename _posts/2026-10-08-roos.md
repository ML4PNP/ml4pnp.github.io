We are happy to welcome **Roos** as a new **Research Assistant** in the **Machine Learning for Precision Neuropsychiatry (ML4PNP) Lab** at Tilburg University.

Roos is currently a third-year Bachelor's student in **Cognitive Science and Artificial Intelligence** at Tilburg University. She will support data collection for our **[NWA-funded P600Norm project](https://www.nwo.nl/projecten/vqlab92202)**, *“Normative Range of P600 Morphology as a Diagnostic and Prognostic Tool in Neuropsychiatric Disorders.”* The project aims to establish normative ranges of the P600 brain response using EEG, with the long-term goal of supporting diagnostic and prognostic applications in neuropsychiatry.

Through her role, Roos hopes to gain **hands-on experience in scientific research**, from conducting experiments and collecting data to understanding how research is carried out in practice. She sees this opportunity as an important step towards her longer-term ambition of pursuing a PhD.

Outside her studies, Roos enjoys a wide range of creative activities, including woodworking, sewing, and skateboard art. She also practices parkour in Tilburg and enjoys exploring new challenges and learning new skills.

**Welcome to the team, Roos!** We look forward to working together and wish you a great start to your research journey.
