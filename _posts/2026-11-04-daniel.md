We are happy to welcome **Daniel-Mihai Pocatilu** as a new **Student Research Assistant** in the **Machine Learning for Precision Neuropsychiatry (ML4PNP) Lab**.

Daniel is currently pursuing his Master's degree in **Cognitive Science and Artificial Intelligence** at Tilburg University. His research interests lie at the intersection of **machine learning, privacy, fairness, and reliable AI**, particularly when working with sensitive real-world data.

At ML4PNP, Daniel will contribute to the **[NPASS Prediction project](https://research.tilburguniversity.edu/en/projects/predicting-the-nurse-perceived-adequacy-of-staffing-scale-npass-s/)**, which focuses on predicting nurses' perceived staffing adequacy using machine learning. The project aims to leverage routinely collected hospital data to develop predictive models that could support **better workforce planning, nurse well-being, and quality of patient care**.

Before joining the lab, Daniel investigated **fairness in Dutch speech recognition across diverse speakers**. He also brings practical experience as an LLM solution architect, a data science intern at the Municipality of Capelle aan den IJssel, and a teaching assistant at Tilburg University. Daniel's experience in **responsible AI and privacy-aware machine learning** will be a valuable addition to our interdisciplinary research activities.

**Welcome to the team, Daniel!** We look forward to working together and wish you a great start at ML4PNP.
