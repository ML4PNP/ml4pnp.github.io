I’m a student at Tilburg University, currently in my 3rd year of the Cognitive Science and Artificial Intelligence bachelor’s program. During my studies, I have learned about cognitive neuroscience, biological psychology, machine learning, and deep learning. At the same time, I have developed my data analysis and programming skills. Although my academic journey is still early, the knowledge and hands-on experience I have gained so far have strengthened my interest in neuroscience and how our brains operate in today’s society. I hope to learn more about the overlap between the brain and computers and how they could complement each other in the future. I don’t yet know exactly  where I will be in a few years, but I am determined to keep learning and developing my skills so I can discover technologies, patterns, or techniques that improve people’s lives.
