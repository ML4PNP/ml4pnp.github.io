I am a master’s student in Cognitive Science and Artificial Intelligence at Tilburg University and a student assistant in the Machine Learning for Precision Neuropsychiatry (ML4PNP) Lab. My research interests lie at the intersection of machine learning and sensitive real-world data, with a focus on building AI that is private, fair, and reliable. Before joining the lab, I investigated fairness in Dutch speech recognition across diverse speakers, resulting in a first-author paper at Conversations 2026. My background includes experience as an LLM solution architect, a data science intern at the Municipality of Capelle aan den IJssel, and a teaching assistant at Tilburg University. Alongside my studies, I founded pdm.ai, an on-premises private AI workspace designed so organizational data never leaves the local environment.
